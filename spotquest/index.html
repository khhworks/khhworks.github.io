Before you begin

A few useful details.

Do I need a wallet to play?

No. Guest mode lets you play without connecting a wallet. Some achievements, including the Sovereign boss badges, are exclusive to wallet mode.

Can I play offline?

Included and downloaded scenes can be played offline. New scene downloads and wallet connection need internet access.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your phone. Guest and wallet profiles have separate progress. Clearing the game's storage or deleting and reinstalling it can erase that progress. Connecting a wallet does not provide a cloud backup.

What are the achievement badges?

Badges mark achievements in the game, from mastery milestones to boss victories. This release awards in-game badges and does not offer NFT minting.
