Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Guest and wallet profiles have separate progress. Wallet connection is not cloud backup. Uninstalling or clearing game storage may erase progress and badges.

Do I need an internet connection?

Included and previously downloaded scenes work offline. Wallet connection and new scene downloads require internet access.

Can I claim an NFT here?

No. This website is informational. The current SpotQuest release awards in-game badges and has no NFT minting. Archived artwork and metadata do not provide a claim service.

What if a wallet shows a warning?

Close the request and contact support with the wallet name, app version and warning text. Never share wallet secrets. A website migration does not automatically remove a wallet's reputation warning.
